# Ancient Greece - sample

#### Scene 1 (0:00 – 0:12)
* **Voiceover:** High above the city of Athens stands the Parthenon, a temple of white marble built to honour the goddess Athena. For more than two thousand years, it has watched over the people below.
* **Video Prompt:** Stick figure citizens in tunics walking up the stone steps toward the Parthenon on the Acropolis hill at golden hour, the city of Athens spread out below.

#### Scene 2 (0:12 – 0:26)
* **Voiceover:** Down in the busy marketplace, a barefoot old man named Socrates spent his days asking questions. He carried no books and wrote nothing down, yet his questions would change the way the world thinks.
* **Video Prompt:** The ancient Athenian agora marketplace in warm afternoon light, stone colonnades, market stalls with amphorae and baskets of olives, a clear open square in the middle.
* **Popups:** socrates = an old barefoot bearded philosopher with a bald head in a simple white toga, one finger raised as if asking a question @ Socrates; student = a curious young student in a blue tunic holding a wax tablet, looking puzzled @ questions; scroll = a rolled parchment scroll tied with a red ribbon (prop) @ wrote

#### Scene 3 (0:26 – 0:38)
* **Voiceover:** In 490 BC, a messenger ran from the battlefield at Marathon all the way to Athens with news of victory. His legendary run is why we still race marathons today.
* **Video Prompt:** An exhausted stick figure messenger running along a dusty road between olive trees, sweat flying, a dropped shield and helmet by the roadside.
